## Charts

1. **25 Years of Container Rates — Shocks & Recoveries** — dual-axis line chart; 13 extreme events plotted directly on the rate curve, COVID peak $11,377/40ft
2. **Container Rate Shocks — 3 Months Before vs After** — diverging bar chart; the COVID escalation chain (+30% → +61% → +61%) and the 2022 deflation
3. **Disruption Landscape — 58 Events in 25 Years** — 8 disruption families by frequency; geopolitical events lead with 12
4. **Recovery Time by Family** — policy & tariff disruptions take 17.4 months to recover vs 1.9 for natural shocks
