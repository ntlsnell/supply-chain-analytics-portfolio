## Charts

1. **Taiwan Made Two-Thirds of the World's Advanced Chips** — stacked area of ≤7nm wafer capacity by country; the US wedge appears only in 2024–2026 as CHIPS Act fabs ramp
2. **Chip War Escalation: Volume vs Severity** — control counts as bars, average severity as a line; the 2022 spike to severity 9.0 is the October BIS package
3. **The AI Boom Did Not Lift Everyone** — slope chart of segment revenue 2019 → 2024; GPU +930% crosses CPU −26%
4. **Sanctions Worked Narrowly, Missed Broadly** — China's leading-edge capacity against its total capacity build-out
