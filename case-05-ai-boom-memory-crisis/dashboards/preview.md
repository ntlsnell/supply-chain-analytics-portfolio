## Charts

1. **As Inventory Drained, Memory Prices Multiplied 5×** — dual-axis scissors chart; price climbs from $10.87 to $60.33 per GB as inventory falls from 14.8 to 3.1 weeks, the lines crossing in mid-2025
2. **HBM Demand from AI Chips vs Consumer Memory Prices** — the cross-dataset result: HBM demand derived from AI shipments plotted against consumer memory pricing
3. **HBM Premium Grew from 48× to 384× Commodity DRAM** — the divergence between the two memory markets, with a trend line showing the 2026 plateau
4. **Enterprise Pays 1.8× per GB at Every Generation** — segment pricing across DDR4, DDR5 and DDR6 preview
